CONTRACT FOR CIVIL WORKS PACKAGE C7
Elevated Viaduct — Edapally to Kakkanad Section
Ref: KMRL-CON-2026-0441  |  Date: 21 August 2026  |  Page 1 of 14
1. Parties to the Contract
This Agreement is entered into on the 21st day of August, 2026, between Kochi Metro Rail Limited (KMRL), a company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013, having its registered office at Metro Bhawan, Ernakulam, Kerala — 682 011 (hereinafter referred to as the "Employer") and Kerala Construction Corporation Limited (KCC Ltd), having its office at Civil Station Road, Thrissur, Kerala — 680 001 (hereinafter referred to as the "Contractor").
2. Scope of Work
The Contractor agrees to execute and complete the Civil Works for Package C7 comprising construction of elevated viaduct structures spanning 4.7 km between Edapally Junction and Kakkanad IT Hub stations, including all associated pier foundations, pre-stressed concrete box girders, bearings, and expansion joints, strictly as per the drawings, specifications, and schedules forming part of this Contract.
3. Contract Value & Payment
3.1 The Accepted Contract Amount is INR 342,00,00,000 (Rupees Three Hundred Forty-Two Crore), subject to additions and deductions in accordance with the Contract.

3.2 Payment shall be made within 30 days of receipt of certified interim payment certificates, as per the schedule of payments at Appendix B.
4. Time for Completion
The Works shall be completed within 36 months from the date of the Letter of Acceptance, i.e., by 21 August 2029. Time is of the essence.
5. Performance Security
Within 28 days of the Letter of Acceptance, the Contractor shall furnish Performance Security of 10% of the Accepted Contract Amount (INR 34.2 Crore) in the form of a bank guarantee from a nationalised bank, valid until 60 days after the Defects Notification Period.
